# Community Code of Conduct

HPI welcomes people across backgrounds, countries, disciplines and viewpoints. Discussion must remain respectful, evidence-focused and considerate of people directly affected by poverty.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, identity-based discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, exploitation of vulnerable participants, repeated personal attacks and misleading claims of institutional endorsement.

Challenge hypotheses and methods robustly; do not attack people. Declare conflicts. If a contribution contains sensitive data or presents risks of real-world harm, maintainers may temporarily restrict it while assessing safeguards.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict access where necessary, documenting material moderation decisions with due regard for privacy. A safe private reporting channel and appeals procedure must be developed before scaling the community. Until then, avoid posting sensitive complaints in public Issues.

This is an initial community standard for review; it is not a substitute for a future independent safeguarding policy.
