01 / OUR MISSION

Progress begins when we measure what matters.

Our ambition is to reduce poverty and expand real opportunities. Rather than promise a perfect economic system, we want to investigate specific problems, test promising approaches responsibly and share reliable findings.

04 / PROPOSED ROADMAP

Long-term vision. Careful first steps.

These are aspirational milestones, not funded commitments. Scientific quality, ethical review and community input matter more than arbitrary deadlines.

NOW / FIRST 90 DAYS

Lay the foundations

Publish the charter, extend our evidence map, agree measurement standards and narrow the first research question.

YEARS 1–3 / ASPIRATIONAL

Evaluate a first intervention

Only with qualified partners, resources and appropriate independent ethical review.

YEARS 3–10+

Replicate and learn

Grow evidence and investigate broader economic mechanisms with open methods.

OPEN KNOWLEDGE, CAREFUL PRACTICE

Scrutiny is a contribution.

We publish research openly, including uncertainties and contradictory evidence. We do not publish sensitive participant records or claim a proven impact before evaluation. HPI is a working name, not an incorporated foundation, charity or research institution.
