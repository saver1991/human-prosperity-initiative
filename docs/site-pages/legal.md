# Legal, publisher & accountability status

**Last updated: 12 November 2026.** Human Prosperity Initiative (HPI) is currently a **provisional, international public-interest research initiative**. It is **not an incorporated foundation, registered UK charity, company, public authority or verified legal partnership**.

## Publisher status

HPI public research and software are maintained openly through the [HPI source repository](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative). There is an [editorial governance draft](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/governance) and a [public work roadmap](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/roadmap). Neither establishes a legal board, legal company, registered office, corporate tax number or appointed charity trustees.

**An accountable real-world site operator, legally sufficient publisher/contact disclosures and relevant jurisdiction-specific requirements have not yet been fully verified.** We will not invent corporate registration numbers, addresses, email inboxes or legal officers. Formal publication/compliance decisions remain tracked in [WEB-03.6](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/89) and [F0-04 governance readiness](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/6).
