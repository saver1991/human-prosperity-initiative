# About the Human Prosperity Initiative

**Working name. International scope. Early-stage open research.** Updated 12 November 2026.

Human Prosperity Initiative (HPI) is exploring ways to make access to prosperity more measurable, equitable and sustainable. Our focus connects **near-term poverty reduction and practical opportunities** with **longer-term research into economic systems**.

## What we're doing now

We publish research protocols, public data source references, critical methodological reports, synthetic research tools and an auditable public roadmap. We are studying whether a carefully defined Pension Credit access problem could be meaningfully addressed in Newham, London. **This remains desk research; no actual participant has been recruited and no HPI intervention outcome has been established.**

Start with [Mission](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/mission), [Research](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research), [Newham prepilot](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/newham-prepilot) or the [Roadmap](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/roadmap).

## Our current organisational status

HPI is **not incorporated**, is **not** a registered charity, company or foundation, and does **not** claim a legally appointed board, statutory partner or independent research-ethics approval. We do not invent contributors' credentials or named roles. The open [governance draft](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/governance) and [charter](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/mission) are not yet fully ratified.
