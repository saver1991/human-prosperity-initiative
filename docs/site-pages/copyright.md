Credit **Human Prosperity Initiative contributors**, link to the source and identify modifications when reusing eligible HPI materials.

## Other people's materials

Third-party studies, government statistics, raw datasets, images, logos, brands, quotations, web links and externally hosted resources **retain their own applicable licences and rights**. HPI's citation or linking to them does not license their redistribution or commercial reuse. Check the original publication, licence, attribution, personal-data and database rights before copying or adapting.

For specific source rights uncertainty, consult the [Source Catalogue](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/source-catalog). Its dataset candidates are references, **not** hosted datasets whose reuse is universally cleared.

“Human Prosperity Initiative” is a working name. No independently verified trademark registration or exclusive mark claim is asserted on this page. Any future branded licensing or IP assignment must be supported by actual agreements.
