# Contact, corrections & support availability

**Last updated: 12 November 2026.** HPI is an open research initiative, **not a staffed service desk**, registered organisation, government advice agency or confidential safeguarding team.

## Available for non-sensitive public collaboration

For software bugs, reproducibility issues, corrections to public source citations and other information that can safely be shown to everyone, see [Get involved](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/contribute), our [public activity tracker](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/activity), and the [HPI public GitHub repository](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative). GitHub requires its own account for some actions, has independent terms, and submissions may remain publicly searchable.

This is **not** a private inbox and cannot guarantee an individual reply or a response deadline.

## Channels that are not yet established

A human-owned **confidential privacy rights-request inbox, safeguarding channel, private participant contact route and verified general organisational address** are still **not established/authorised**. No email address, phone number or support team is invented. These must be set up with real ownership, access controls, retention and legal review before accepting sensitive cases.

**Do not post personal benefit details, identity numbers, children's information, medical data, financial hardship records or other sensitive documents on GitHub or through this site.** HPI has not authorised participant recruitment or personal-data fieldwork.

## If you need help now

For immediate danger use the emergency services available in your own location. For social security and benefits advice, start with [official GOV.UK Pension Credit guidance](https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit) or a qualified local service. HPI cannot provide individual entitlement decisions.

If the concern is with personal-data handling, see the [ICO complaints guidance](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) (UK regulator). Your rights to complain to a regulator do not depend on HPI creating a private contact facility.
