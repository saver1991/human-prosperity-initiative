# Help & frequently asked questions

**Last updated: 12 November 2026.** This is self-service information. HPI does not yet operate a staffed helpdesk, welfare advice service, emergency or confidential safeguarding hotline.

## What is HPI?

HPI is an **early-stage, unincorporated and open** research initiative exploring measurable ways to reduce poverty, increase access to prosperity and, over time, investigate how economic mechanisms might work better. See [Mission](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/mission) and the [Roadmap](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/roadmap).

## Has HPI helped people receive benefits?

**No independently verified HPI human-beneficiary impact has been established.** A Newham study is currently **public-source desk research only**, not a live intervention. The [Newham prepilot](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/newham-prepilot) explains what is known and why fieldwork is on hold.

## Where do I find projects and tools?

- Start at [Research](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research) for studies and available evidence; [Roadmap](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/roadmap) for future programmes and technical statuses.
- [Source Catalogue](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/source-catalog) searches **39 source references and 3 dataset candidates**, *not* 42 verified full datasets.
- [Synthetic Outcome Lab](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/outcome-lab) uses fictional group numbers. **Do not enter personal financial or benefits details.**
- [Reproducibility](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/reproducibility) shows sources and reproducible technical reports.
- [Responsible Tools](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/responsible-tools) describes the ethical and data-rights constraints.

A new **Projects and Tools homepage is planned**, not yet available. Existing direct links above work today.

## Can I apply for Pension Credit through HPI?

**No.** HPI is not a government service, advice agency or eligibility assessor. For official benefits information use [GOV.UK Pension Credit](https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit), or seek qualified local welfare advice.

## How do I contribute or report a factual correction?

Read [Get involved](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/contribute) or [Research integrity](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research-integrity). GitHub discussion/issue contributions are **public**; they are useful only for non-sensitive technical and scientific comments. Actual community-participant review requires a separate, human-owned protected process; it has not started.

## Is anyone available for private support?

A verified HPI private contact and safeguarded support channel **has not yet been established**. Please do **not** send personal hardship, health, identity, banking or family records to public GitHub. For urgent personal risk, contact appropriate local emergency or professional services; HPI is not an emergency responder.

More information: [Contact & feedback](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/contact).
