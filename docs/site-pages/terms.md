# Site terms, limitations & responsible use

**Last updated: 12 November 2026. Status: plain-language interim usage guidance; formal binding terms need an identified operator and legal review.** HPI is a working public-interest initiative, not an incorporated charity or foundation.

## What HPI provides

The website shares open research ideas, versioned source documents, methodological critiques, synthetic calculations, public dataset *references* and a public roadmap. Content may be provisional, disputed, corrected or superseded. A successful technical build does not mean an intervention has been independently approved or has improved anybody's prosperity.

## What it does not provide

- **No personalised Pension Credit, welfare, legal, financial, medical or tax advice.**
- **No official government service or representation of Newham Council or the DWP.**
- **No approved human research participant recruitment, case processing or safeguarding hotline.**
- **No guarantee that proposed studies or tools establish a causal treatment effect.** For example, the [Newham desk study](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/newham-prepilot) concerns public modelled aggregate figures, not confirmed individual eligibility.

If you need actual financial or benefit advice, use a relevant authorised professional or official service, not the Synthetic Outcome Lab.

## Using and contributing materials

Software source is made available under the [MIT licence](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/library/software-license); original HPI documentation is generally under [CC BY 4.0](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/library/documentation-license) unless its file says otherwise. **Third-party datasets, quotations, imagery and linked publications are not automatically licensed for redistribution.** See [Copyright & licences](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/copyright).

Public contributions must follow the [Code of Conduct](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/library/code-of-conduct). Do not publish confidential information, personally identifying benefit cases, private documents or any information about minors in public GitHub conversations. We may correct or remove public submissions according to project rules and service-provider controls, but cannot promise complete erasure of third-party cached copies.
