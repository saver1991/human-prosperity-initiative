# Research integrity, sources & corrections

**Last updated: 12 November 2026.** HPI's scientific and ethical programme is preliminary. We publish open, traceable source material and record negative findings, source uncertainties and the distinction between technical work and real-world welfare effects.

## What HPI currently knows

The [NEW-02 official Newham source reconciliation](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/new-02-newham-source-reconciliation-additionality-v0-2) checked a published **2023/24 DWP model**: around **11,300** potentially eligible Newham households, **8,800** modelled recipients, **2,500** potentially eligible nonrecipients and **78% modelled household receipt**. A separate **86%** concerns expenditure receipt, **not the same denominator**. A GB-wide **62%** survey-based benefit-unit rate refers to a different year and design; it must **not** be combined into a simple comparison.

None of these figures is an HPI cohort result, verified claimant eligibility, first payment, incremental council benefit, cause-and-effect proof or a study participant dataset. The first HPI outcome denominator and numerator remain **NULL** and any field study is **NO-GO**.

## Challenging or correcting a claim

A public, **non-sensitive** correction should identify the exact HPI page, passage, source URL, original publication date, the claim's interpretation and possible counterevidence. See [Get involved](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/contribute). Issues/PRs may remain public indefinitely; **do not** include anyone's personal benefit, medical or identification data. For a private complaint or safeguarding matter, a staffed private channel is **not yet available** through HPI.

Independent scientific critique, community involvement and ethics must be obtained from real qualified people before any claim of V1.0 scientific approval or study impact. Track the [review gate](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19) and [protected community gate](https://github.com/svtmzt/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/82).
