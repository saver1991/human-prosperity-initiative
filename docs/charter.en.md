# Human Prosperity Initiative — Founding charter (English public summary)

> **Draft v0.3 — 5 November 2026.** HPI is a working name, not a registered foundation. The [Italian charter](charter.it.md) is the more detailed migrated source document. This English summary is intended for international collaborators.

## Vision and mission
**Vision:** A world where everyone can access the conditions for a dignified, secure and sustainable life.

**Mission:** Create and openly share rigorous research, models and practical solutions to reduce poverty and expand opportunity while deepening our understanding of underlying economic mechanisms.

## Two connected programmes
- **Applied programme, priority for ~0–5 years:** evidence mapping, partner discovery, ethical trials or other appropriate evaluations, measured outcomes and replication.
- **Systems research, starting now with limited resources:** explore and challenge the structural processes generating insecurity and unequal opportunity; publish explicit hypotheses, assumptions and model limitations.

An initial 80/20 applied/systems allocation is a planning *hypothesis*, not a binding budget.

## Prosperity dimensions
1. Economic security and resilience.
2. Essential services, adequate shelter, food, energy and water.
3. Health and education.
4. Real economic and social opportunity.
5. Autonomy, safety and intergenerational sustainability.

Do not collapse these into a single unvalidated score. Compare relevant established measures and specify local outcomes.

## Impact and research standards
- Specify primary and secondary outcomes, baseline, comparison, duration, cost, equity impacts and possible harm.
- Prefer designs with credible causal inference; randomise only where ethical and feasible.
- Follow informed-consent, privacy, data protection and independent ethical oversight requirements.
- Publish methods and findings—including null or negative results—with appropriate confidentiality safeguards.
- Distinguish **reach** from **demonstrated benefit**.

## First milestones
**0–30 days:** charter review, measurement specification, initial research evidence map and risk log.
**31–60 days:** compare three problem areas and initiate partner exploration.
**61–90 days:** choose a candidate problem and formulate a falsifiable study concept; progress only after go/no-go gates.
**1–3 years (aspirational):** one independently evaluated, properly authorised pilot; scale only after credible evidence of impact and acceptable costs/risks.

## Governance
Founding coordination is provisional; there is no incorporated institution or appointed independent board. Scientific independence, transparent decisions, community participation and disclosure of conflicts of interest are prerequisites of institutional growth.
