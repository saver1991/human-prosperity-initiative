# ADR-0002 — Proposed accountable governance for an unincorporated HPI

**Date:** 5 November 2026. **Status: Provisional policy for public review, not legal constitution or an appointed board.** Related: [F0-04 #6](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/6).

## Context

The project has a public research mission and a single founder-operated repository, but no incorporated institution, validated intervention, formally appointed independent scientific/community board, approved research protocol, private welfare reporting route or fundraising authority. Published science and future study participants need stronger protections than a standard open-source project's contributor guidelines.

## Provisional operational direction

- Maintain existing founder custodianship for repo and operational issues **only**, with branch checks and auditable decisions.
- Distinguish technical merge authority from scientific/ethical approval. Require conflicts and recusals for material decisions, preserve negative findings and reviewable dissent.
- Create public decision logs, legal-options comparison and independent/community review criteria; do not imply reviewers are appointed.
- Prohibit donations, unapproved partner agreements, individual case data, participant outreach or human-subject research until legitimate legal, ethical and safeguarding arrangements exist.
- Keep personal welfare complaints out of public GitHub while a properly staffed secure channel is not established.

## Options assessed

1. **Claim an immediate independent foundation:** rejected as false and legally unsafe.
2. **Founder-controlled code merges count as scientific review:** rejected; creates an obvious independence conflict.
3. **Provisional transparent governance with explicit unfilled gates:** selected as a **documentary proposal**, subject to community critique and an attributable future governance decision.

## Consequences and open approvals

The GitHub-maintained rules can guide non-sensitive document/engineering operations. They are **not** a licence for participant research, fundraising or legal contracts and do not appoint trustees. Competent independent/community/legal review and actual secure-reporting capacity remain pending. Before a study or incorporation, adopt the revised rules in the appropriate legal form with real responsible people and record conflicts/decision authority.
