# F0-04 — Scientific and community review model (proposal)

**Status:** proposed operating model, not evidence of a staffed committee. HPI currently has no appointed independent researchers, community representatives, ethics officer or safeguarding lead.

## Independent methods review

Before upgrading a candidate measurement framework to validated study protocol, invite reviewers who can assess causal identification, statistics, distributional effects, costs, measurement instruments and data governance. Record each actual reviewer role, scope, potential funding/affiliation conflicts, dissent and independence relative to the draft authors. One contribution should never be presented as unanimous expert consensus. Do not claim peer review solely from AI-assisted critique.

## Community input with real decision influence

Require a plan that explains why affected people should review the outcome definitions; who is excluded by language, disability, connectivity or time burden; compensation/reimbursement, when legal and budgeted; how dissent could change the candidate study; and how to avoid coercion or misleading promises. **No consultation of vulnerable individuals should begin until appropriate local partner, ethics, safeguarding and secure data collection arrangements exist.** Public GitHub Issues are for non-sensitive method criticism only.

## Review workflow

1. Publish a versioned draft, questions and open uncertainty register.
2. Invite non-sensitive public contributions, ensuring accessible summary/translation.
3. Before any real human consultation, obtain the legal/ethical/safeguarding capability and authorised local partner.
4. Record actual comments and conflicts; classify accept/revise/decline with documented reasons.
5. Confirm an independent sign-off decision (or mark missing and retain draft status).
6. Freeze an approved method version for each ethically authorised study, then publish deviations and negative outcomes transparently.

## Non-negotiable methodological controls

- No single global prosperity score or invented population/impact estimate.
- No unsupported causal inference from application rates or activity counters.
- Harm to nonparticipants and unequal subgroup effects must be investigated rather than hidden in averages.
- Explicit sample, source, local validity, power/precision, attrition, missingness and data protection.
- No funder/founder right to alter analyses or suppress negative findings.
- No private respondent or service-eligibility records in public Git or unapproved Neon datasets.
