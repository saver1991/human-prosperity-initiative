## 2. Funding instruments and budget relevance

- **Grant / restricted gift:** permitted only via lawful recipient and donor restrictions; ring-fence geographical, outcome, staff and overhead terms; not automatically core cash. A committed restricted grant is not unrestricted reserves.
- **Unrestricted gift / core grant:** useful for governance and independent open research if legally permitted and verified; no actual donor, legal recipient or unrestricted receipt currently exists.
- **Public procurement / research-service contract:** commercial deliverables, procurement rules, VAT/tax and negotiated IP and publication rights; **not grant aid**. A UNGM tender belongs here.
- **In-kind expertise, software credits or service donations:** separately documented and consented; not cash and no guaranteed budget savings.
- **Mission-related investment / equity:** would require separate lawful entity and governance strategy; no assumption that ordinary shares belong within a CIO.
- **Academic fiscal/research administration:** an eligible existing university or charity might contract, apply or hold funds **only with its own authorised approval**. The existence of an institution on our research landscape is not an endorsement, partnership, willingness to host or ability to use it as a bid lead.

Link any future bid to the illustrative [Lean / Base / Expanded cost scenarios](F0-04-4-operating-budget-and-sustainability-v0.1.md), real unrestricted runway and approvals. **Zero confirmed funds, £0 authorised organisational spending, no grant applications.**

**Evidence and accountability.** Public versioned research, transparent negative/null outcomes, external reviewer conflicts and dissent, safeguards before human contact and explicit international local-law gates.
