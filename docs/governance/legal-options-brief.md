# F0-04 — Future legal form and operating boundaries (exploratory)

**Status:** Desk analysis only, 5 November 2026. HPI is an individual-led public repository, **not** registered as a charity, foundation, nonprofit, company or research sponsor. This is **not legal advice** and does not conclude HPI qualifies for charitable status.

## Trigger: do not incorporate merely to create a website

A suitable legal form becomes important if HPI needs to sign research agreements, hold participant-related liabilities, accept grants, hire people, lease property, provide services or establish durable community-governed ownership. First assess mission eligibility, country of activity, fundraising routes, required insurance and permissions, independent trustee/adviser availability, accounting obligations, compliance cost and realistic funding.

## Shortlist for future examination

| Form / jurisdiction | Benefits in principle | Limitations / diligence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England and Wales **charitable incorporated organisation (CIO)**: association or foundation model | Separate legal personality; suitable for charitable purposes; association model enables wider membership and foundation model governance by trustees | Charity eligibility, regulator registration, constitution, genuinely independent responsible trustees, reporting, charitable-purpose/public-benefit review, tax and international operations need expert evaluation |
| England and Wales **charitable company limited by guarantee** | Separate legal personality and potential grant/contract capacity | Dual company/charity obligations where registered, governance/admin cost, formal directors/trustees and public filing obligations |
| **Unincorporated association / trust** (where appropriate) | Potentially lower entry complexity in limited activities | **Not a separate legal person**; trustees/members may bear personal contractual liabilities; a poor default for research contracts, salaries or participant-facing risks |
| Nonprofit/public-benefit vehicle in a study jurisdiction | May better align with local partners, local funding, language and ethics review | Local counsel needed; residency, governance, financial, cross-border transfer and tax rules vary; international operation does not itself choose legal domicile |
| Remain a public open research collaboration | No premature institution, no claim of registration, low immediate admin burden | Cannot act as registered HPI legal person, cannot safely contract/fundraise/sponsor a participant study in that name; founder remains repository custodian |

Sources: [Charity Commission CC22a structures](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charity-types-how-to-choose-a-structure-cc22a), [UK government structural changes and unincorporated limits](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/change-your-charity-structure), [Charity Commission CC29 conflicts, updated 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/identifying-and-managing-conflicts-of-interest-in-a-charity-cc29).
