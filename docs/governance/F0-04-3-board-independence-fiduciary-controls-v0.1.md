# F0-04.3 — Independent governance, board and fiduciary controls (draft V0.1)

**Prepared:** 7 November 2026 · **Classification:** public documentary / pre-incorporation readiness only · **Authority:** proposal for founder, qualified counsel and future independent trustees to challenge.

**No statutory charity, board, trustees, bank account, appointed safeguarding lead, grant authority, independent methodological reviewer, fiscal sponsor or ethics committee currently exists.** HPI currently has a founder-administered public research repository. This document is *not* adopted articles, binding financial policy, charity registration, an external endorsement or permission for field research, public donation solicitation, partner contracts or transfers.

**Scope:** F0-04.3 [#48](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/48); consistent with [provisional governance V0.2](../../GOVERNANCE.md), [legal options F0-04.2](F0-04-2-legal-structure-and-international-objects-v0.1.md), [budget F0-04.4](F0-04-4-operating-budget-and-sustainability-v0.1.md), [safeguarding F0-04.1 #26](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/26) and [independent science review F0-03.1 #19](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19).

## 1. Model and accountability

**Draft recommended pattern:** small qualified *future* board (illustratively 3–5 human trustees if an England/Wales CIO is ultimately chosen), separation of fiduciary oversight from the founder/operator, and separately engaged scientific and community advisers. The actual constitution, composition, minimum trustee count, quorum, committee powers, appointment requirements and voting rules must be selected consistently with the Charity Commission's model foundation/association constitutions **and signed off by real trustees/legal counsel**. A foundation CIO's members are its trustees; an association CIO allows wider voting membership. A fiscal host has its own legally accountable board and requires a different governance agreement.

| Proposed function | Minimum capability to recruit/check | Independent oversight / boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board chair / governance | Charity public-benefit duties, meeting discipline, conflict handling and accountability | Challenge founder; cannot self-certify an independent appointment |
| Finance / treasurer | Accounting, budgets, controls, restricted funds, fraud, reserves | Reports to unconflicted board; not sole payee and authoriser |
| Scientific/evaluation trustee or adviser | Evaluation design, causality, open reproducibility, null/negative results | Independent protocol and results critique; not automatically an ethics board |
| International operations / law | Foreign partners, local permission, cross-border risk, grant due diligence | Country-specific local counsel required before actual operations |
| Lived-experience/community participation | Locally relevant, non-extractive inclusive input, accessible alternatives | Participation voluntary, fairly safeguarded; avoid tokenism or disclosure of hardship |
| Safeguarding/data lead | Welfare escalation, complaints, privacy, lawful data transfer | **Not appointed**; implementation governed separately by #26 and real human ownership |

**Provisional design threshold, not an existing legal rule:** target at least 3 genuinely eligible trustees, a majority free from material founder/sponsor conflicts, and an unconflicted quorum of at least 2 for material decisions; where conflicts reduce quorum, stop and seek independent/legal resolution. Counsel and a future constitution may require a different lawful quorum. Trustees remain collectively accountable even when they delegate.

## 2. Decision rights (future state vs now)

| Decision class | Current founder-run public project | Future institution — proposed gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public docs, citations, tests, releases | Founder/maintainer may approve with source trace and CI | Delegated technical maintainer within board-approved policy |
| Research methods, estimands, results | Publish as provisional; **no independent approval claimed** | Independent methods challenge, ethics where relevant, versioned dissent; board cannot alter scientific conclusions for donor preference |
| Beneficiaries, actual recruitment, personal data | **STOP** | Lawful country/partner/ethics/data-controller authorisations plus safeguarding channel, trained human decision-makers |
| Legal registration, fiscal host, contracts, banking | **STOP — no HPI legal power** | Explicit founder incorporate/host/hold decision #51, counsel and genuinely authorised legal signatories |
| Budget, spending, restricted funds, fundraising | No public funds or HPI contract; planning figures only | Budget approved by valid board/host, authorised payment roles, income controls, donor eligibility and restrictions verified |
| Country deployment, local partners | Desk research only | Per-country risk review F0-04.6, sanctions and permissions, written agreements, monitoring and stopping rules |

**Founder boundary:** sole technical custody does not imply powers of trustees, scientific validators, data controllers, or funds trustees. No related-party contract, pay, proprietary licensing or transfer of assets can be silently approved by the founder. Any remunerated founder role must be independently assessed under the final legal form, conflicts policy, charity rules and tax advice, with recusal and publication of the permitted decision.

## 3. Conflict, independence and agenda protocol

1. Maintain a private detailed register and a minimised public declaration summary for each trustee/adviser, including financial, family, employer, sponsor, donor, ownership, university, research and connected-party interests. Record *unknown* rather than "none" when not checked.
2. Request declarations on appointment, annually, and as an explicit agenda item before each material matter. Record the actual matter, declaration, decision, abstention, recusal and resultant lawful quorum.
3. For material conflicting transactions, first consider **removing the conflict or not proceeding**; do not merely have a conflicted person sign an approval. Validate trustee benefit permissions, terms, constitution and Charity Commission authority where necessary.
4. Separate author, funder, outcome assessor, methods reviewer and final decision-maker where possible. External peer review is genuinely independent only with real human expertise, conflict disclosure, source-specific critique and attributable disposition.
5. Funder conditions must never suppress negative findings or control recruitment, consent, eligibility or interpretation. Where an independent body cannot be arranged, label outputs *unreviewed* and stop any approval-dependent action.
6. Preserve dissent, recusal, version history and publication of negative/null results when lawful, without disclosing sensitive contact, complaint or participant information.

Grounding: Charity Commission [CC29](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/identifying-and-managing-conflicts-of-interest-in-a-charity-cc29/conflicts-of-interest-a-guide-for-charity-trustees) (updated 22 April 2026); [CC22a](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charity-types-how-to-choose-a-structure-cc22a).

## 4. Draft financial and fiduciary control design

**Present hard rule:** HPI does not operate a legal entity's funds. No donation checkout, sponsorship offer, money transfer, trustee payment, debt, grants or reimbursements in HPI's name should be enabled under this draft.

**Only after establishment/host approval:**
- Board/host adopts annual balanced budget, documented 12-month forward cashflow, reserve policy, named budget owners, segregated accounting for each restricted grant, accounting reconciliation, monthly performance review and quarterly risk review. Treat the lean/base/expanded amounts in F0-04.4 as **scenario assumptions, not commitments**.
- Controls must require at least two separately authenticated real humans for new bank details and outward payments (preparer + independent authoriser), with independent bank statement reconciliation and permission review. Do not permit a person to propose, authorise and reconcile the same transaction.
- **Illustrative policy thresholds, NOT operative:** under £500 within an approved budget = named budget holder proposal and different authorised approver; £500–£4,999 = treasurer/finance reviewer and independent authorised approver; £5,000+ or any unbudgeted, cross-border, connected-party or restricted-fund exception = disinterested board/host approval before commitment. Adjust thresholds to scale and bank controls with qualified finance advice; the current spending limit remains **£0**.
- Pre-acceptance donor/grant checks: lawful source, sanctions, reputation, conflicts, scope of restrictions, right to publish results, data conditions, foreign obligations, reporting burden and exit/return-of-funds rights. Reject any donor veto over independent science or illegal/incompatible donor restriction.
- Procurement: proportional competition/price comparison; explicit single-source justification and connected-party recusal; documented quote/evidence; no splitting transactions to avoid approval levels.
- Anti-fraud/bribery: accessible whistleblowing route with human owner and anti-retaliation, conflict declarations, suspicious-transaction review, incident escalation, documented financial loss and required authority reports. No public incident log with sensitive details.
- Separate accounting for restricted/unrestricted funds, gift/grant/contract/ordinary investment, and avoided assumptions about Gift Aid, donor tax relief or charity tax status. Never market a funding source as guaranteed.

Grounding: Charity Commission [CC8 financial controls](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/internal-financial-controls-for-charities-cc8/internal-financial-controls-for-charities), [CC26 risk management](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charities-and-risk-management-cc26/charities-and-risk-management-cc26).

## 5. Safeguarding, research ethics and complaints — separate approval gates

This pack does not implement a private reporting system and **does not close #26**.

**Before human-contact activity:** independently appoint actual safeguarding lead and deputy, restricted-access confidential channel, locally appropriate immediate/emergency referral protocol, retention and access log, subject-rights process, complaint/appeal and allegations management. Test the channel synthetically and with real accountable owners before any claims of readiness. Public GitHub is for non-sensitive critique, **never** benefit case histories or welfare allegations.

**Before human research:** real responsible organisation/data controller(s), contract/processor and international transfer review, local ethics clearance, non-extractive community consultation, genuine independent methodological review per #19, harm monitoring and documented stop conditions. An automated test, internal AI review or founder assent cannot replace any human approval.

## 6. Recruitment, ratification and records (not performed)

Proposed sequence — all **pending actual people and decisions**:
1. Founder records legal-form decision or HOLD under [#51](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/51), and counsel reconciles the model CIO constitution or host agreement with HPI's charter and decision rights.
2. Publish accessible role profiles, transparent selection criteria, skills/gap assessment and safeguards for independent/lived-experience participation; do **not** publish personal application details.
3. Human-led eligibility and independence due diligence, voluntary consent, identity/fitness where legally required, sanctions/disqualification checks appropriate to role, declarations and documented appointment by authorised body.
4. Inaugural lawful board/host meeting: constitute quorum, adopt conflict, finance, complaints, grant, procurement, risk and safeguarding policies, approve budget and bank mandates, sign minutes, establish review dates. Legal counsel to verify any required registration first.
5. Record future real decisions in [decision-template](decision-template.md), with date, authority, votes/recusals, rationale, evidence, minority view, version and review cadence. Publish redacted policy outcomes, not private personal information.
6. Annual governance self-assessment, independent challenge, fund-flow audits and revision; do not equate a GitHub issue closure with actual trustee appointment.
