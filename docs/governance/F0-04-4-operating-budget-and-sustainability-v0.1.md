# F0-04.4 — HPI 12–24-month operating budget, resilience and funding model V0.1

**Prepared 7 November 2026. All amounts GBP. Illustrative planning assumptions, NOT vendor quotations, grant offers, board-approved budgets, invoices, cash balances, audited accounts or commitments.** HPI is not yet an incorporated organisation. This document does not authorise donations, payroll, procurement, grant applications, field research or legal incorporation.

## Executive view

Three scenarios span the difference between a volunteer-led *research desk programme*, a modest paid organisational team with independent researchers, and a multi-country expanded operation. **None includes launching a real Newham benefits experiment or paying participants**. Such a programme requires a separate protocol, safeguarding, lawful data, local partner and ring-fenced study budget.

| Illustrative 3-month recurring cost buffer incl. contingency | £4,125 | £27,600 | £93,600 |
| Setup + buffer, incl. contingency, **opening liquidity screen** | £8,525 | £41,400 | £135,600 |

**Do not add the 3-month reserve again to the year-1 expense total:** the reserve is a *cash liquidity stock* held alongside operating expenditure, not a new expense. The 'opening liquidity screen' is a **cash adequacy threshold**, not a 12-month operating funding requirement or fundraising target. A steady-state 3-month unrestricted buffer is only an illustrative internal starting point, not a Charity Commission rule; a future board must justify any reserve policy using risk and actual unrestricted funds.

## 2. What a financing plan actually distinguishes

| Instrument | Who can receive / spend | Spend constraints and budget role | Unverified as of 7 Nov 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unrestricted core donation/grant** | Only lawful legal entity/host with authority | If permitted, governance, core operations, reserves and qualified charitable work | **£0 committed; no entity/host** |
| **Restricted programme grant** | Eligible entity/host under binding funder terms | Geographic/programme activities only; approved overhead allocation if funder permits; **not automatic free reserves** | **£0 awarded; Newham fieldwork not approved** |
| **Research-service contract** | Contract-capable host or entity | Cost and revenue earned under deliverables, VAT/tax, IP and independence terms | **No customer, pipeline conversion or contract** |
| **In-kind donated expertise/cloud** | Donor and receiving institution/individual by written terms | Reduces cash need but creates capacity and dependency risk; measure donated service separately, do not assume market-price cash | **No donor commitment** |
| **Founder personal funds** | Individual chooses separately; not HPI cash, no presumed reimbursement | No legal obligation, not automatically a donation, no open-ended personal exposure | **£0 approved** |
| **Equity investment** | Potential *commercial company* only, not ordinary share capital of a CIO | Separate later business/hybrid choice, governance/conflict and mission safeguards | **Out of scope / no investor** |

**Key finance logic:** expected unrestricted receipts `U` cannot include restricted `R` except for approved contract/award overhead recovery `O` expressly permitted by agreement. For a no-deficit core scenario: `U + O + actually drawable starting unrestricted cash >= planned core cash spending + required minimum liquidity`. Track programme budgets separately; no double-counting the same award in unrestricted and restricted ledgers.

A real project might need multi-currency accounting, restricted grant sub-ledgers and project cost attribution (Newham, Recife etc.). **No foreign programmes are funded here.** Charities' free reserves must reflect available unrestricted resources and appropriate exclusions; consult current [Charity Commission CC19 (updated September 2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charity-reserves-building-resilience-cc19).

## 3. Startup, runway, zero-award and release controls

**Zero-funding scenario:** if no confirmed lawful host/entity funds and no explicit personal commitment, **authorised paid spending is £0**. Continue solely with donated-time public research and tools under free authorised tiers; cancel/avoid optional subscriptions and do not sign liabilities. The full 'Lean' scenario is an **aspirational funded** operation, **not** a cash balance or permission to spend.

**Funding sequence (proposed):**
1. **Month 0 — desk only:** source audit, governance options and budget, no paid obligations.
2. **Before any payable commitment:** identify accountable entity or fiscal host, lawful invoicing and bank facilities, donor restriction terms, procurement authority, cash funds actually accessible and signatory approvals.
3. **Before hiring or project launch:** verified *unrestricted* runway for committed recurring core cost and planned one-offs, and separately ring-fenced programme cash for approved intervention.
4. **Monthly review:** cashbook, restricted fund reconciliation, grant milestone liability, receivables age, actual/plan by project, 13-week rolling cash forecast, unpaid obligations, incident reserve and a documented stop/escalate condition.
5. **Quarterly review:** refresh cash runway, reserve adequacy, salary/FX/inflation sensitivity, open source sustainability, negative/positive scientific outputs and grant eligibility.

**Runway is defined only on confirmed usable cash:** `unrestricted liquid balance ÷ forecast net monthly unrestricted burn`, where burn and balance are based on real accounting records (not a pledged grant or hypothetical scenario). Until bank balances and contracts exist, **runway = not measurable**, not 0 months and not infinite.

**Suggested stop triggers (future board to ratify):** do not start a recurring contract unless its full cancellation liability is funded; pause uncommitted work if 13-week forecast cannot cover payroll and required liabilities; flag below 3-month internally targeted **available unrestricted** buffer; trigger professional response if charitable spending, grant terms, tax filings or local data/ethics permissions are unclear. Do not automatically terminate safeguarding obligations if operations wind down.

## 4. Procurement, financial controls and auditability

- **Two-person rule** for future nontrivial payments, bank details changes, high-risk foreign disbursements and related-party contracts; set actual monetary thresholds only when a real board/host approves. A GitHub/CI bot cannot be a statutory approver.
- Source attribution per expense: amount, FX/date, original receipt, vendor, budget line, unrestricted/restricted fund code, payer and authoriser, benefit/project purpose, conflict declaration and bank settlement reference. Do not store personal bank details or beneficiary records in the public repository.
- Quote comparison and conflicts: use proportionate competitive quotations, independent review of founder-related services, written award conditions and verified end-use evidence for overseas transfers.
- Monthly/annual reporting: independently reconcile bank/cash, recognise liabilities under applicable accounting policy, verify fund restrictions and update the reserve policy. Auditor / independent examination requirements vary with legal form and income/assets and **changed in 2026**; re-check at the real reporting date. [Charity accounts 2026 threshold table](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charity-accounts/threshold-changes-at-a-glance).
- For project impact, **report actual costs, verified programme outputs and independent welfare outcomes separately**. Transfer of a statutory benefit to a claimant is **not** the organisation's operating spend or proof that the organisation caused the claim.

## 5. Sensitivity and explicit omissions

The biggest uncertainties are: actual trustee/host overhead, legal advice and regulated research sponsorship, qualified statistician rates, staff and employer on-costs, London versus overseas researcher rates, whether Newham public-interest partners would participate, data-controller infrastructure, interpreters/offline access and independent ethical review. The monthly values are **not externally priced forecasts**.

The model does not forecast revenue, assume awards, calculate an invented benefit-cost ratio or assign a fictional first-study sample size. Adding **an authorised real pilot** requires its **own** participant-safe, partner-approved incremental cost and funding plan including ethical compensation (if any), participant support, legal privacy infrastructure, governance, follow-up and adverse-event provisions.

### Scenarios and downside

- **Lean:** zero salaried labour; low cash need but heavy donated-time concentration and possible inability to secure actual independent review at assumed prices. If research expertise is not donated, lean may be **operationally infeasible**.
- **Base:** fractional organisational coordination and meaningful independent methods budget; requires sustained **real** unrestricted funding (year-1 cash need **£124,200**, not just £8,000/month). Does not finance a field study.
- **Expanded:** not an immediate recommendation; presupposes genuine multiple-country programmes and future trustees' readiness. **£416,400 first-year illustrative cash costs** without field-study allocations is a reason **not** to scale early.
- **FX / inflation:** 24-month nominal totals assume flat costs; in real use a scenario must rebase for inflation, tax and legal obligations. Contingency is an arbitrary planning buffer, not a forecast inflation rate.
