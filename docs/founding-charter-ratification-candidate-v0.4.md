# F0-01 — Founding Charter V0.4 (candidate for public ratification)

**Status: CANDIDATE / NOT RATIFIED — 5 November 2026.** Human Prosperity Initiative (HPI) is a **working name** for a public-interest, open-source research initiative; it is **not** an incorporated foundation, registered charity, legal person, medical provider, research sponsor or certified institution. Its provisional founder administers this GitHub repository; that role does **not** confer independent scientific validation. The more detailed [Italian V0.3 draft](charter.it.md) and [English public summary](charter.en.md) are background; this text is a **proposed bilingual-review baseline**.

## 1. Vision

A world in which **everyone** can access the conditions for a dignified, secure, free and sustainable life, without one person's gain depending on avoidable impoverishment or coercion of others.

## 2. Mission

Advance, critically test, and openly share methods, evidence, models and practical interventions that reduce poverty and expand people's genuine opportunities; in parallel, investigate how fundamental economic and institutional structures affect the distribution and sustainability of prosperity. Publish failures, null results, trade-offs and limits as clearly as positive findings.

**We do not assume a predetermined political ideology, economic model or universal policy winner.** HPI judges interventions by transparent evidence, affected-community experience, opportunity cost, equity and safeguards.

## 3. Two connected research horizons

**Horizon A — Applied improvement, near-term priority.** Identify an actionable problem supported by sound evidence, assess alternatives, collaborate with appropriately qualified and authorised local partners, and evaluate whether any proposed intervention changes real participant outcomes safely and durably. A rigorous study within approximately three years is an *aspiration*, not a funded or authorised promise. **No pilot, beneficiary recruitment or delivery commitment has been approved.**

**Horizon B — Economic-systems research, longer-term.** Build falsifiable explanations and reproducible models of economic institutions, poverty mechanisms and constraints on opportunity. Clearly distinguish descriptive evidence, simulations, predictions and empirical causal estimates. This horizon starts in exploratory form but cannot displace Horizon A without an explicit public decision and capacity justification.

The previously suggested **80:20 attention split** is a planning hypothesis, never a fixed spending rule or validated optimisation.

## 4. Prosperity and outcomes

Prosperity means effective ability to live with dignity and security, meet essentials, exercise agency and access opportunities **without shifting significant harms to other people or future generations**.

Five *working*, overlapping domains: (1) economic security and resilience, (2) adequate essentials, (3) health and education, (4) economic and social opportunity, (5) agency, safety, fairness and environmental/intergenerational sustainability.

The [F0-03 measurement V0.1](../research/F0-03-prosperity-measurement-framework-v0.1.md) and [indicator catalogue](../research/F0-03-indicator-catalog-v0.1.csv) provide **candidate** measures only. There is **no approved single HPI Prosperity Score, validated weighting, approved study outcome or measured impact**.

Distinguish *people reached*, *verified service access*, *people demonstrably helped* and *lasting net effects*, and distinguish participant improvements from externalised costs, displacement and harms to nonparticipants. Do not sum overlapping benefits as separate gains.

## 5. Scientific and ethical commitments

- Select one clear, falsifiable primary question and defensible participant outcome per study (or justified co-primary outcomes with multiplicity controls), with units, denominators, baseline, comparator, observation horizon, missingness, effect uncertainty, subgroup equity, cost and adverse events specified in advance.
- Prefer credible counterfactual methods where feasible and ethical, but **never deny a lawful entitlement or essential care to create a control group**. Do not equate before/after associations with causal effects.
- Make evidence searches reproducible and include negative/contradictory results, risk-of-bias evaluation, competing explanations and local transferability limits. [F0-02 evidence work](../research/F0-02-opportunity-assessment.md) is exploratory, **not** yet a systematic review.
- Involve affected communities meaningfully without extractive research, deceptive claims, pressure to disclose hardship, or token representation. Independent ethics/legal/safeguarding review is required where applicable before fieldwork.
- Collect the minimum lawful data for any authorised purpose. Never publish identifiable participant records. In the UK, consent to **participate** in research and an appropriate **UK GDPR lawful basis** for data processing are different decisions; both must be evaluated for the particular study. [ICO research guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/principles-and-grounds-for-processing/).
- Preserve study protocols, uncertainty, independent criticisms, code and reviewable aggregate findings, subject to privacy, security, rights and third-party licensing. A source being available publicly does not confer redistribution rights.
- Do not claim an intervention or institutional design works unless the relevant method, evidence, costs and safety support that conclusion. Independent replication is preferable before scaling.

## 6. Selecting the first applied challenge

Initial *candidate* directions are (A) verified access to existing benefits/services, (B) affordability of essentials and (C) sustainable livelihoods. **No location, population or provider has been selected.**

Following F0-02 research, use the original six-criterion evidence rubric: severity of need **20%**, credible impact evidence **20%**, feasibility/cost **20%**, ethics and safeguarding **15%**, additionality **15%**, measurable learning **10%**. These are provisional decision weights, **not impact weights for prosperity**. Exclude any option with unavoidable serious harm, no lawful route to required data or an unacceptable ethics risk *regardless of numerical score*. Publish assumptions, disagreement and explicit go/no-go.

## 7. Open science, authorship and community

Use the current repository's applicable licences for *original* contributions, with attribution, version history, corrections and reproducible methods. External data, third-party articles and proprietary material keep their owners' rights. Invite critical assessments, community participants, researchers, translators, accessibility specialists, public-interest practitioners and people with lived experience. Do not describe open GitHub contributions as formal employment, appointment to a board, consent to a human-subject study or membership of an incorporated institution.

## 8. Provisional governance and decision rights

- The founder/repository owner can manage technical access, moderation and provisional project planning, but **may not certify their own scientific results as independently validated**.
- Material changes to mission, charter, outcome definitions, research protocols, safeguards or legal status must have a public proposal and a reasoned decision record; preserve credible dissent.
- Contributors declare relevant conflicts of interest, recuse themselves from incompatible decisions and must not silently remove negative findings. Neither funding nor founder preference can veto publication of scientifically sound inconvenient findings.
- An independent research reviewer and community adviser structure is **proposed**, not appointed. No external person has approved this candidate.
- Before receiving funds, signing institutional commitments, engaging vulnerable groups or commissioning a human-subject study, determine the appropriate legal entity, ownership, delegated authority, financial safeguards and independent ethics/legal obligations. No funding solicitation or participant intake is authorised by this charter.

See [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) and the public decisions register.

## 9. Approval, change control and unresolved questions

This candidate becomes **Charter V1.0 only after**:
1. A public review window with at least one credible, documented opportunity to challenge the English and Italian texts (not fabricated comments).
2. Explicit decisions on inconsistencies, conflicts, vulnerable-community safeguards, ownership and public-facing claims.
3. Independent/community input where appropriate, or a candid statement of which types of review did **not** occur.
4. A recorded, attributable founder/advisory ratification decision and changelog in GitHub, **not** merely a successful CI build or PR merge.

Unresolved at V0.4: formal legal structure/jurisdiction, advisory members, funding and expenditure authority, secure welfare reporting channel, initial country/partner/population, validated instruments and any ethics or research data-processing authorisation.
