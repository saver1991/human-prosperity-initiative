**Status:** v0.3 proposal, November 2026. Timing is indicative and depends on collaborators, funding, research quality and legal approvals.

|---|---|---|---|

**Priority:** Practical reduction of poverty and expanded access to prosperity. **Long-term:** Better understand and potentially improve fundamental economic mechanisms. Not opposing goals.

## First 90 days
**Days 1–30:** Public repo and charter; evidence inventory; criteria and risk register.
**Days 31–60 (reprioritised 6 November 2026):** F0-04 institutional and funding desk planning is P0 NOW. Geography shortlisting research can be retained, but no final country or participant decision until founder reviews institutional options. Independent stakeholder outreach is not authorised automatically.
**Days 61–90 (conditional):** Record the real founding/fiscal-host decision and legal/financial review gates; only then resume final population selection and a pre-pilot concept, subject to further independent ethics and partner approvals.

**Not yet decided:** geography, target population, pilot partner, legal structure, funding model, validated composite metric. No participant recruitment should occur without ethics and data governance.

## Operating model — Now / Next / Later

HPI follows **Product / Programme → Initiative → Epic → Task**, with scientific milestones, decisions and risks tracked separately. This is the same planning grammar used across the broader ecosystem, adapted to a **public-interest research initiative** rather than a commercial product. HubOS contains HPI as a non-commercial public-interest programme (production database registration verified 5 November 2026). SolOS provides an independently permission-gated, read-only project and evidence view; this public repository remains the source of truth for scientific research and community work.

### F0-04 — Institutional, legal and funding readiness (P0 · NOW · PLANNING ONLY)

The founder reprioritised HPI's organisational/legal/financial foundation ahead of final geographic intervention selection on **6 November 2026**. The commercial versus charitable form is expressly **undecided**; a later comparison is registered as **F0-04.8 #54**. HPI aims to be **international**, even if ultimately constituted as an England/Wales CIO. Charity Commission guidance allows UK charities to work globally while requiring local legal compliance, partner due diligence, safeguarding and accountability for funds. No form is selected, no charity is incorporated and no grant/partnership application or human outreach is authorised.

The [full founding/funding workplan](governance/F0-04-founding-and-funding-readiness.md) specifies six P0 epics: [F0-04.2 legal entity and charitable objects #47](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/47), [F0-04.3 board and controls #48](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/48), [F0-04.4 budget and sustainability #52](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/52), [F0-04.5 grants/partners #49](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/49), [F0-04.6 international operations #50](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/50), and [F0-04.7 founder legal formation decision #51](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/51). Existing [F0-04.1 safeguarding #26](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/26) remains mandatory. HPI GitHub is the task authority, [SolOS #94](https://github.com/saver1991/solOS-core/issues/94) is a read-only operational projection plan, and [HubOS #130](https://github.com/saver1991/the-hub-os/issues/130) tracks portfolio governance.

**F0-02.3 stays an unapproved desk shortlist**. This change neither selects UK/Brazil/Italy beneficiaries nor represents financial, legal or scientific approval.

### Continuity from original roadmap V0.1

The original six phases remain represented; later versions refined the priorities and introduced stronger scientific controls.

| Original V0.1 phase | Placement in V0.3 |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | F0-01 and initial F0-04 |
| 2. Open-source infrastructure | Public GitHub, Vercel, Neon, CircleCI; tracked as infrastructure work such as [WEB-01](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/9) |
| 3. Global scientific community | F0-04, open contribution model and independent review |
| 4. Economic modelling | Horizon B systems research within F2; preliminary hypothesis development can start earlier |
| 5. Scientific validation | F0-02 methods, F0-03 measurements, F0-05 design, F1 real-world evaluation |
| 6. Experimentation | F1, only after independent review, ethical approval and feasible partners |

**Decision:** Near-term poverty reduction and wider opportunity remain the applied priority; systems research remains a second, connected long-term horizon. We do not treat a simulated economic model or a published website as proof of improved human outcomes.
