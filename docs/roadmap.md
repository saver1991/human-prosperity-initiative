**Priority:** Practical reduction of poverty and expanded access to prosperity. **Long-term:** Better understand and potentially improve fundamental economic mechanisms. Not opposing goals.

## First 90 days
**Days 1–30:** Public repo and charter; evidence inventory; criteria and risk register.
**Days 31–60 (reprioritised 6 November 2026):** F0-04 institutional and funding desk planning is P0 NOW. Geography shortlisting research can be retained, but no final country or participant decision until founder reviews institutional options. Independent stakeholder outreach is not authorised automatically.
**Days 61–90 (conditional):** Record the real founding/fiscal-host decision and legal/financial review gates; only then resume final population selection and a pre-pilot concept, subject to further independent ethics and partner approvals.

**Not yet decided:** geography, target population, pilot partner, legal structure, funding model, validated composite metric. No participant recruitment should occur without ethics and data governance.

HPI follows **Product / Programme → Initiative → Epic → Task**, with scientific milestones, decisions and risks tracked separately. This is the same planning grammar used across the broader ecosystem, adapted to a **public-interest research initiative** rather than a commercial product. HubOS contains HPI as a non-commercial public-interest programme (production database registration verified 5 November 2026). SolOS provides an independently permission-gated, read-only project and evidence view; this public repository remains the source of truth for scientific research and community work.

### F0-03.2 — Newham Pension Credit desk validation V0.2 (technical feasibility delivered)

The founder selected **Newham, London** for a bounded public-source **measurement validation test**, initially examining verified access to Pension Credit among potentially eligible pension-age households. Four official/council *context* figures have been pinned with dates, provenance, units and disjoint denominators; **all 15 draft F0-03 indicators have a contextual feasibility disposition**. The [public Newham readout](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/newham-pension-credit), [scientific desk report](../research/F0-03-newham-pension-credit-desk-validation-v0.2.md) and [GitHub F0-03.2 #68](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/68) document why actual verified Newham eligible/recipient numerator and denominator remain **unknown** and why council service outputs cannot be credited to HPI.

This is a **completed technical desk test, not F0-03 scientific V1.0 validation or fieldwork**. [Independent methodology and community review #19](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19), [data/ethics decision gates #67](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/67), F0-04 legal/finance and F0-05 go/no-go remain open. The proposed scientific question must add knowledge beyond Newham's established Our Newham Money programme or be halted.
