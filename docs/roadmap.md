# Roadmap — Human Prosperity Initiative (working name)

**Status:** v0.3 proposal, November 2026. Timing is indicative and depends on collaborators, funding, research quality and legal approvals.

| Stage | Horizon | Deliverable | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| F0-01 Founding charter | Nov 2026 | Draft charter, values, scientific principles | Community review and v1.0 approval |
| **F0-02 Evidence and opportunity assessment** | 0–60 days | Rapid evidence scan, shortlist, evidence ledger, opportunity assessment | Sources validated; gaps and ethical risks recorded |
| F0-03 Prosperity measurement framework | 0–60 days | Outcome taxonomy, baseline and measurement guide | Independent methodological review |
| F0-04 Governance and contributor operations | 0–90 days | Decision log, contribution review, safeguards and future legal-options brief | Transparent ownership, code of conduct |
| F0-05 First study concept | 61–90 days | Problem statement, theory of change, planned evaluation and feasibility | Partner, ethics, budget and data access before any pilot |
| F1 Applied impact | 1–3 years | First ethically approved and independently assessed field study (aspirational) | Credible effect estimation, costs and harms |
| F2 Replication and systems research | 3–10 years | Reproducible work, further studies, alternative models | Transferability and external review |
| F3 Wider institutional impact | 10–20+ years | Research-informed interventions adopted more widely | Ongoing evidence and democratic legitimacy |

**Priority:** Practical reduction of poverty and expanded access to prosperity. **Long-term:** Better understand and potentially improve fundamental economic mechanisms. Not opposing goals.

## First 90 days
**Days 1–30:** Public repo and charter; evidence inventory; criteria and risk register.
**Days 31–60:** Expand evidence mapping, engage stakeholders, score candidate contexts only when data are available.
**Days 61–90:** Select one specific population/problem and write pre-pilot concept note with go/no-go criteria.

**Not yet decided:** geography, target population, pilot partner, legal structure, funding model, validated composite metric. No participant recruitment should occur without ethics and data governance.

Track actionable work in [Issues](../../issues).

## Operating model — Now / Next / Later

HPI follows **Product / Programme → Initiative → Epic → Task**, with scientific milestones, decisions and risks tracked separately. This is the same planning grammar used across the broader ecosystem, adapted to a **public-interest research initiative** rather than a commercial product. HubOS contains HPI as a non-commercial public-interest programme (production database registration verified 5 November 2026). ### Continuity from original roadmap V0.1

The original six phases remain represented; later versions refined the priorities and introduced stronger scientific controls.

| Original V0.1 phase | Placement in V0.3 |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | F0-01 and initial F0-04 |
| 2. Open-source infrastructure | Public GitHub, Vercel, Neon, CircleCI; tracked as infrastructure work such as [WEB-01](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/9) |
| 3. Global scientific community | F0-04, open contribution model and independent review |
| 4. Economic modelling | Horizon B systems research within F2; preliminary hypothesis development can start earlier |
| 5. Scientific validation | F0-02 methods, F0-03 measurements, F0-05 design, F1 real-world evaluation |
| 6. Experimentation | F1, only after independent review, ethical approval and feasible partners |

**Decision:** Near-term poverty reduction and wider opportunity remain the applied priority; systems research remains a second, connected long-term horizon. We do not treat a simulated economic model or a published website as proof of improved human outcomes.
