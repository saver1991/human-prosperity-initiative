## Review questions for contributors

1. **Substance:** Does the definition of prosperity exclude or undervalue any essential capability, right, group or future-generation interest? Cite a specific text section.
2. **Scientific standard:** Which statement could be interpreted as an unsupported causal/effectiveness claim? What counterevidence or design correction is needed?
3. **Equity and dignity:** Would this process inadvertently burden nonparticipants, create coercion or require digital/language access some communities lack?
4. **Governance:** Are decision rights, conflict recusal, scientific independence and dissent-handling credible while GitHub remains individually owned?
5. **Legal/ethics:** Are data-processing, partnership, fundraising and future legal-status boundaries explained without suggesting HPI is registered?
6. **Translation:** Are the English candidate and Italian V0.3 draft materially consistent on values, horizons, 5 domains and first-study safeguards? Identify exact sections, not general impressions.
