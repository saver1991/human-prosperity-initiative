## Assess each dimension using evidence, not impression

Score **0 = not defensible**, **1 = important revision needed**, **2 = sufficiently specified for this provisional stage**, or **N/A = not applicable / insufficient evidence**. A score is a **methodological audit**, not a prosperity score or study approval.

## Required narrative assessment

1. **Top three risks of false causal claims:** specify affected sentences, conditions and a safer interpretation.
2. **Most important omitted harm or excluded group:** explain why the measure may be misleading in its absence.
3. **Which candidate primary endpoint is not currently measurable or valid, and why?**
4. **What evidence would change your conclusion?** Include contrary research and null results where possible.
5. **What human or jurisdiction-specific review is required that no AI/code CI can provide?**
6. **Recommended action:** accept provisional catalogue as review-ready / revise and resubmit / unsuitable without new context. **None of these approve a real human-subject study.**

## Responses and publication rule

HPI records actual independent responses in issue [#19](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19) or a linked PR, with attribution/COI and a line-by-line method-change disposition. Revisions remain provisional until a documented qualified independent review and affected-community participation are obtained where ethically/legally feasible. Separate secure safeguarding/data channels are prerequisites for collecting personal feedback. Preserve dissent without revealing sensitive identities.
