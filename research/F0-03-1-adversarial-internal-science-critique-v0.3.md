**Critical verdict:** **NO-GO for causal efficacy or a live pilot** under current evidence. The mathematical outcome HPI-B-01 is undefined for HPI participants and there is no unique service additionality. A descriptive evidence-provenance project could be defensible; a claimed benefit-delivery intervention would currently be unsupported.

## Falsification findings (author-internal, not externally adjudicated)

| AI-IR-01 | Critical | The DWP's Newham 11,300 denominator represents *modelled area households* (2023/24), not legally verified HPI participant benefit units. Therefore `8,800/11,300` cannot be HPI's success rate, or a baseline risk for power. | A lawfully ascertained actual study denominator, eligibility dates, confirmation of nonreceipt and paid first awards. | **BLOCK** participant outcome |

| AI-IR-03 | Critical | HPI may duplicate Our Newham Money, displace staff attention and increase applicant burden, producing zero or negative incremental welfare. | Independently verifiable gap in existing services and a genuine incremental, low-burden benefit to residents. | **BLOCK** new outreach |
