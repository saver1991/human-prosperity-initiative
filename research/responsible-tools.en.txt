HPI — Responsible research and access, version 1.0 (6 Nov 2026)
Public source: https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/responsible-tools

Public HPI research tools currently permit public reference metadata and explicitly fictional scenarios only.
DO NOT submit personal data, pseudonymised records, identifying poverty stories, beneficiary details or unlicensed source documents.
No real human study has been authorised; no scientific intervention benefit has been demonstrated.
Unknown copyright/data rights, re-identification risks, legal controller/basis, ethics, consent, international rules and real screen-reader accessibility require independent human review.
Software MIT, HPI-authored documentation CC BY 4.0; neither licences third-party data.
For detailed rules and all eight pending gates, see docs/RT-01-4-responsible-research-and-accessibility-v1.md in the public Git repository.
This text file can be printed and used offline. It is not legal advice or an approved fieldwork protocol.
