# F0-02.3 — Comparative desk shortlist of jurisdictions and potentially affected populations

**Version:** V0.1 for founder discussion, 6 November 2026.
**Decision status:** **PROVISIONAL RESEARCH SHORTLIST — NOT APPROVED**. This document records a bounded public-source *desk mapping* of **three** contexts, proposes **two** for discussion, and explicitly does **not** select a pilot site, target participants, intervention or partner. No organisations/individuals contacted, no participant data downloaded, no funding or new data-sharing agreements sought. The final geographic and population choice requires founder review; field research requires separate ethical, safeguarding, institutional and legal approvals.

## 1. Research question and selection guardrails

Where might HPI credibly evaluate barriers between **formal entitlement/access** and **verified benefit receipt**, while not duplicating already established services, and with a reasonable chance of later measuring genuine household prosperity gains?

This is a geographic/population *feasibility comparison* informed by [F0-02.1 research protocol](research-protocol.md) and [F0-02.2 critical evidence synthesis](F0-02-critical-synthesis-2026-11-06.md). Benefit take-up remains an **exploratory lead**, not HPI's approved domain. Energy affordability and economic opportunity remain available alternatives.

### Inclusion and evaluation rules
1. Search **official, non-identifying** sources available by 2026-11-06; distinguish local data from national and regional proxies. Keep actual search execution and source URLs visible.
2. Require a concrete administrative/service pathway, a plausible population with unmet need and public macro/aggregate evidence; record existing service providers to avoid duplicating their work.
3. Compare observed need, **data availability**, plausible causal outcome measurement, legal/ethics, accessibility and language, partner opportunity, incremental HPI value, and cost/operational complexity.
4. Use **qualitative labels only**, not unsupported international ranking or 1–5 weighted scores. Different poverty statistics use different denominators and definitions and **must not be compared numerically** between jurisdictions. F0-02.5 formal scoring and intervention selection stays separate.
5. No named partner is an agreed collaborator. Public statistics are not a legal basis to access case-level recipient or application data. HPI currently has no authorised field project or identified research institution.

## 3. Candidate A — Newham, England (conditional shortlist)

**Official evidence**
- [English Indices of Deprivation 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/english-indices-of-deprivation-2025/english-indices-of-deprivation-2025-statistical-release): Newham **39.0% income deprivation** and **46.7% income deprivation affecting older people**. **These are not household absolute poverty rates or the proportion unclaimed Pension Credit.**
- [DWP Pension Credit FYE 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/income-related-benefits-estimates-of-take-up-financial-year-ending-2025/income-related-benefits-estimates-of-take-up-financial-year-ending-2025): **62% GB take-up**, up to **900,000** entitled pensioner families not claiming, up to **£2.4bn** unclaimed **in GB**, not Newham.
- [DWP ad hoc local-authority eligible/non-recipient analysis for 2023–24](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/households-potentially-eligible-for-pension-credit-2023-to-2024): promising subnational baseline, **local Newham point estimate not extracted/validated** in this mapping, and earlier vintage than FYE 2025 national estimates.
- [Our Newham Money — council service](https://www.newham.gov.uk/advice-support-benefits/newham-money): free debt/benefit/income-maximisation advice, meaning a new generic benefit navigator risks **duplicating** existing public assistance; [Council benefits services](https://www.newham.gov.uk/advice-support-benefits/benefits-income-maximisation).
- [DWP existing Pension Credit outreach](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2025-12-04/97229) includes work with Age UK and Independent Age; **no HPI relationship exists**.

**Potential question, not intervention commitment:** Do **existing** assisted outreach and follow-up routes lead to verified new Pension Credit **awards/payment and household net income** among difficult-to-reach eligible older residents, relative to current practice? Can differences be measured without intrusive collection, algorithmic eligibility decisions or withholding legal entitlements?

**Data plan:** use published deprivation, national take-up, and local-modelled estimates for desk baseline; **do not invent Newham nonclaimant counts**. Identify a lawful local provider and counterfactual before any individual outcome analysis. For official benefit awards, negotiate only with competent lawful data controllers if a reviewed study is approved.

**Risks:** language accessibility, digital exclusion, possible immigration/status complexity for some groups, stigma, inaccurate eligibility advice, duplicate existing DWP/charity campaigns, and *UK GDPR* research safeguards ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/principles-and-grounds-for-processing/)).

**Go/no-go questions:** Does the DWP borough-specific 2023–24 table permit a reliable baseline? Is there an incremental evaluation question not already answered? Could a legitimate public/charitable delivery partner independently approve study/data access? If **no**, do not promote this candidate to F0-05.

## 4. Candidate B — Recife, Brazil (conditional shortlist)

**Official evidence**
- [IBGE Recife municipality](https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/pe/recife.html): 2022 population **1,488,920** (not poverty count). Municipality's published population estimates and regional indicators are a starting context, not evidence of benefit non-take-up.
- [Bolsa Família eligibility and actual entry](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/receber-o-bolsa-familia): CadÚnico registration and monthly household per-capita income threshold **R$218** can make a family **eligible to be considered**, but **entry is not automatic** and depends on federal assessment/budget. Do not frame non-award as a service failure without distinguishing eligibility, data-quality issues, quota and programme decisions.
- [Recife Conecta — CRAS/CadÚnico service](https://conecta.recife.pe.gov.br/servico/352): registration/update with municipal CRAS, digital booking and offline service channels. Existing service presence confirmed; effectiveness not assessed.
- [MDS municipal VIS DATA / reports](https://www.gov.br/mds/pt-br/orgaos/SAGICAD/dados-e-ferramentas-informacionais/dados-e-ferramentas-do-cadastro-unico/) and [Recife city CadÚnico open-data description](https://dados.recife.pe.gov.br/dataset/cadastro-unico-para-programas-sociais): a **2023** anonymised person-level source with 2026 catalogue update. The 2023 vintage, deidentification and presence/absence of outcomes **must be inspected** before research use. **No download conducted** in this phase.
- [LGPD identified-data sharing limits](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/lgpd/cessao-de-dados-do-cadastro-unico-para-realizacao-de-estudos-e-pesquisas): identified CadÚnico research data sharing is described for qualifying **research organisations**, with restrictions including cross-border transfer. **A public GitHub project is not authority to access it**; HPI must not solicit or handle this data.

**Potential question:** Is an unmet gap in existing municipal CadÚnico onboarding/update **measurable** by subsequent actual programme award/benefit receipt (not merely registration)? Only evaluate if local actors can legally distinguish federal selection constraints, incomplete forms and avoid penalising families.

**Data plan:** begin with *aggregated* MDS dashboards and public municipal service mapping. Avoid participant-level data or raw “anonymised” exports until independent privacy/re-identification appraisal. A local lawful research partner, Portuguese-speaking professional safeguarding and explicit outcome access are hard gates for further work.

**Risks:** programme eligibility changes, budget-dependent awards, incomplete/delayed public source, possible reidentification even of “anonymised” files, documentation and internet barriers, family/child sensitive data, and no existing approved partner. Comparability with UK/Italy poverty measures **not claimed**.

**Go/no-go questions:** Is there evidence of service additionality rather than building a copy of CRAS? Is a local, legitimate and independently governed team available? Can real approvals/benefits be tracked **lawfully** and without cross-border personal data transfer? If **no**, limit HPI to open public-data research, not field intervention.

## 5. Candidate C — Naples, Campania, Italy (reserve)

**Official evidence**
- [ISTAT 2025 national poverty report](https://www.istat.it/comunicato-stampa/la-poverta-in-italia-anno-2025/) and [regional official publication reporting Campania **20.6% household relative poverty**](https://www.regione.puglia.it/web/ufficio-statistico/-/istat.-le-statistiche-sulla-poverta.-anno-2025). **Region-level** indicator only, not a city poverty or ADI non-take-up estimate; not comparable directly to Newham and Recife.
- [INPS ADI service](https://www.inps.it/it/it/dettaglio-scheda.it.schede-servizio-strumento.schede-servizi.assegno-di-inclusione-adi.html): eligibility depends on household composition, financial means and other conditions; do not equate low household income with eligibility.
- [INPS June 2026 observatory](https://www.inps.it/it/it/inps-comunica/notizie/dettaglio-news-page.news.2026.08.osservatorio-adi-e-sfl-i-dati-aggiornati-a-giugno-2026.html): **690,000** ADI households **nationally**, average **€672**. Neither figure estimates Naples non-take-up.
- Existing INPS/patronato and territorial social-services structure is an institutional pathway **requiring city-level verification**; no partner endorsed or local caseload verified.

**Potential question:** Could trusted in-person application assistance increase **approved ADI benefit payments** and access equity relative to current channels? **Do not prioritise** until the local eligible/denominator evidence, duplicate-programme assessment and local service mapping improve.

**Why reserve:** no better demonstrated *incremental* research gap yet, despite meaningful regional hardship and a plausible language/administrative pathway. Reconsider if UK/Brazil feasibility fails.

## 6. Side-by-side decision support — qualitative, no false ranking

| Dimension | Newham (UK) | Recife (Brazil) | Naples (Italy) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Need evidence | **Strong** deprivation and national non-take-up, but missing verified current borough-specific take-up | **Strong** active programme pathway and municipal social registry, but *current untreated eligible gap unquantified* | **Strong regional** relative-poverty context; *city ADI eligible gap unknown* |
| Open data | **Good** DWP local analysis + Stat-Xplore; individual recipient outcomes restricted | **Mixed** MDS aggregates; 2023 city dataset and privacy/data rights questions | **Moderate** INPS/ISTAT aggregates, local outcome/denominator gap |
| Existing delivery | Extensive: council and national campaigns → high duplication risk | Federal/state/municipal CRAS system → high duplication risk | INPS, patronati and welfare services → high duplication risk |
| Measurable outcome if approved | New verified Pension Credit awards, income after claim | Verified Bolsa Família awards, actual transfers; budget allocations complicate counterfactual | Verified ADI awards/transfers and retention |
| Ethics and legal burden | UK GDPR, older/vulnerable users, qualified access to DWP records | LGPD, potentially child/household data, research-organisation restrictions and local partner | GDPR, vulnerable families, need lawful INPS records and partner |
| Operational burden | **Lower relative complexity** for English-language desk research; field work still requires partners | **Higher** local-language and institutional coordination | **Moderate/unknown** subject to partner availability |
| Incremental HPI value | Conditional replicable evaluation of a specific missed pathway | Conditional transparent measurement of actual benefits vs only registry completion | Conditional evidence of gap otherwise under-measured |
| Shortlist status | **Discuss option A** | **Discuss option B** | **Reserve** |

**Key methodological constraint:** The UK income-deprivation indicator, Italy's *relative* poverty rate and Brazil's CadÚnico programme reach are fundamentally different measures. **No ranking of poor people, welfare quality or intervention impact can be inferred from these values.**

## 7. Outputs, conditions and decision to discuss

**Researcher-proposed shortlist for F0-02.3:** (1) **Newham**: potentially eligible older low-income residents and Pension Credit; (2) **Recife**: very low-income families facing CadÚnico registration/update and subsequent Bolsa Família award barriers. **Naples remains a credible reserve**, not discarded.

**Why these two?** They test different HPI strategic paths: **nearer-term operational measurability** versus **higher-complexity international systems research**. This is *not* proof of higher impact or superior people/places. Founder can reject both, prioritise Naples, or ask for additional geography.

**No final founder approval requested or recorded in this document.** F0-02.5 weighted six-criterion scoring and the F0-05 pilot go/no-go have not begun. Next steps only after discussion:
1. Founder chooses whether to **investigate one or both provisional candidates further**, or substitute a new region.
2. If approved for further desk work, extract dated local DWP municipality figures (Newham) and verified municipal MDS aggregate programme denominators (Recife), map *existing* interventions and whether a gap truly exists.
3. Separately approve any F0-02.4 ethical partner consultation; contact no one automatically. Before any participant-linked work: independent ethics/privacy and safeguarding review, partner/data controller agreement, language access, funding and outcome measurement plan.
