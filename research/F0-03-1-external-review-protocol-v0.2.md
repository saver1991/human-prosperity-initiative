# F0-03.1 — Independent methods and community review protocol V0.2

**9 November 2026 · PUBLIC, UNSENT, REVIEW-READY PROTOCOL.** This is an invitation *framework*, **not evidence of independent review, appointment, participants, community engagement, ethical approval, legal authority, funding or prospective study registration**. HPI is not a registered research institution or controller of claimant records.

## A. What can be reviewed safely right now

Qualified economists, statisticians, impact evaluators, benefits-administration specialists, accessibility researchers and ethics practitioners may critique **only public methods, published aggregate sources and source-derived metadata**. The [current Newham methods challenge V0.2](F0-03-1-newham-independent-review-readiness-v0.2.md) is the canonical question set; [the original R1–R11 review form](F0-03-independent-review-form.md) remains blank until a real reviewer fills it.

**Safe channel:** a public GitHub pull request proposing methodological corrections to this repository or a comment on [issue #19](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19). The repo and issue are **public and not a confidential submission channel**. Before commenting, reviewers should confirm they are happy for the submitted non-sensitive content to be visible and retained in project history. A non-public review route requires a real appointed human custodian and verified secure safeguarding/privacy process before activation.

**Strict exclusions:** no household financial/benefit/medical records, identifiable narratives, claimant identifiers, private contact details, photos of documents, service complaints naming people, recruitment, interviews, direct-benefits advice or claims of affiliation with Newham Council/DWP.

## B. Independence and reviewer suitability (not yet assessed)

An actual review coordinator (a real appointed person, not a bot) must verify:
1. Relevant qualifications or practice expertise for the specific critique, documented without collecting unnecessary identity data publicly.
2. Any founder, programme, funder, institution, operator or service provider relationship; relevant financial conflicts, coauthorship and potential professional bias.
3. Terms of publication/attribution and any agreed disclosure of conflicting judgments.
4. **Scope:** economic evaluation/methods, data provenance, applied benefits administration, accessibility/ethics and/or community lived-experience perspective. No single reviewer is expected to cover everything.
5. Independence from HPI authorship for any independent scientific sign-off. An AI agent, repository author or internal collaborator cannot certify its own draft.
6. **Human welfare gate:** community input can occur only after independent local safeguards, no coercion, accessible and non-digital options, and a real protected contact/escalation route are established (see #26 and #67).

There is currently no named or engaged reviewer, no appointed study sponsor, no funding or contractual arrangement.

## D. Disposition workflow (do not automate approvals)

1. Register a public non-sensitive submission **after actual receipt**, link it to a source commit and canonical question ID; do not fabricate.
2. Separate supported corrections from open challenges; mark disagreements, adverse/null evidence and uncertainty rather than suppressing them.
3. For each response record accept/revise/reject/unresolved and evidence link, with independent reviewer confirmation when actually obtained. Protect sensitive material off GitHub.
4. If genuine independent methods review and protected affected-community input both exist, consider a documented scientific **V1.0 review decision**. That decision remains separate from legal data access, data protection, ethics and field authority.
5. Until all applicable permissions are recorded by accountable humans, retain **NO-GO_FIELD** and no personal research data in any HPI tool or public repo.

## E. Requested review output and transparent limitations

Minimum output is a signed/attributable or consented pseudonymous *non-sensitive* methods critique with source-specific corrections, confidence, dissent and conflict disclosure, plus reviewer evidence that they actually inspected the material. Independent ethics/privacy and accessible community consultation require different qualified processes. The current internal paper, CI, static pages and reviewer form do not satisfy them.

Source controls: [ICO principles and legal grounds for research processing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/principles-and-grounds-for-processing/) (consult latest guidance; currently under legislative update) and [HRA approval decision guidance](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/approvals-amendments/what-approvals-do-i-need/) (apply only when the future research scope actually falls under its remit). Neither grants HPI permission to handle claimants, nor does the term 'scientific research' automatically establish a UK GDPR lawful basis.
