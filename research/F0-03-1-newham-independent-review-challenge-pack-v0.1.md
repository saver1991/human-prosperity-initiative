## Questions an actual independent reviewer must challenge

**Population and causal inference**
1. Do the statutory Pension Credit rules require a **benefit unit** rather than a household denominator? How and when could independent lawful eligibility and first actual payment be verified?
2. What is the defensible comparison and estimand over existing **Our Newham Money** services? Could a new service simply duplicate and displace current delivery?
3. Would randomisation of **additional assistance**, never of owed benefits, be ethical, justified and logistically feasible? If not, which observational design, pre-trends, assignment or natural experiment could support causal claims?
4. How would attrition, pending claims, differing eligibility dates, repeat applications, referrals, clustering, noncompliance, spillovers and differential missingness be handled?
5. What would be required for a prospectively defensible sample, statistical power, meaningful effect threshold, pre-registration and null/negative result protocol?

**Source validation and measurement**
6. Does Newham's **46.7% income deprivation among residents aged 60+** (IoD 2025) support contextual need without implying any particular person is Pension Credit eligible? (Yes for context, **no for eligibility**.)
7. Why must **39% borough all-age income deprivation** and **62% GB FYE2025 national Pension Credit take-up** never be combined to estimate the local non-take-up rate?
8. Can the DWP **2023/24 modelled local-authority potential-receipt** row be independently extracted, its uncertainty and known missing savings/housing costs verified, and compared only under a defensible same-measure design?
9. Does the Newham council self-report of **220 supported claims and £748,590 income attributed in 2024/25** actually establish unique paid claims, attributable gain or net cost-effectiveness? Which independent audit of overlapping reports and costs is necessary?
10. Are HPI-B-01 and HPI-B-02 appropriate valid measures; what adjustments would be needed for language, literacy, disability, digital exclusion, fairness, claimant burden and measurement invariance?

**Rights, safeguarding, participation**
11. Who is the accountable UK data controller and study sponsor? Which lawful basis, data minimisation, security, access rights, retention and DPIA/ethics requirements apply? Could protected data ever be shared lawfully with this unincorporated initiative? (**Not currently authorised.**)
12. What harms could stem from incorrect advice, delayed benefits, distress, stigma, unnecessary reassessments, leakage, withdrawal from established support or diverting public-service capacity? What independent harm escalation and stop rules are acceptable?
13. How should local older people and qualified community organisations participate **through an approved protected nondigital process** without coercion or publishing identifiable experiences?
14. How will actual native VoiceOver/NVDA/TalkBack and offline access be assessed with people, not assumed from axe or Playwright?
15. What evidence would lead to **NO-GO** for a new HPI programme, including independent proof that existing evaluation already answers the question or that no credible incremental effect can be measured?
