# Research evidence protocol — F0-02 (draft)

**Status:** Scoping protocol proposal; not yet a registered systematic review.

## Question
For people experiencing poverty or economic vulnerability, which practical interventions improve (A) receipt of existing entitlements, (B) affordability of essential costs, or (C) sustainable economic opportunity? Under what conditions, for whom, at what cost, and with what harms or distributional effects?

## Literature identification
Search sources including 3ie Development Evidence Portal, J-PAL, World Bank, UNDP, OECD, IEA, academic databases where available, grey literature and registered trials. Record date, query, database, inclusion decisions, language limitations and duplicate handling in a public search log. Follow PRISMA guidance if proceeding to a formal systematic review.

## Eligible evidence
Prefer impact evaluations, systematic reviews and high-quality synthesis; include policy and administrative documentation separately, never treating descriptive programme counts as effect estimates. Include null/negative effects. Record study year, geography, population, intervention, comparator, outcomes, point estimates and confidence intervals where available, research design, follow-up, costs, conflicts and generalisability.

## Assessment
Use transparent risk-of-bias assessment appropriate to each design. Never pool incomparable interventions without a justified model. Separate actual benefit receipt from application, training participation from earnings, and programme coverage from demonstrated improvement.

## Ethics and privacy
Open science ≠ open participant records. Publish licensed aggregated data only, with independent review and informed consent when required. Never launch a trial or recommend denial of owed support to form control groups.
