# Governance — provisional

HPI is an **early-stage open initiative**, not an incorporated foundation, charity or research institution. The repository presently belongs to an individual GitHub account. No one should claim to represent a legally constituted HPI entity.

## Decision making
- Material strategy and public-facing research protocol changes go through tracked Issues/PRs, with rationale and alternative views documented.
- Founding coordination is temporarily responsible for repository administration and safeguarding; that is **not** scientific validation of its proposals.
- Scientific claims should be traceable to evidence and reviewable by external contributors.
- Disclose relevant financial, professional or institutional conflicts of interest.
- Proposed interventions require appropriate local, legal, data-protection and independent ethics approvals before human participation.

## Future institutional governance
An independent non-profit/foundation is an **aspiration conditional on** community need, accountable governance, funding, legal advice, and a suitable jurisdiction. A community governance charter and transparency plan should be developed before incorporation.

## Protecting participants
Personal data, case histories and sensitive details must never be committed to public git history, including accidentally in Issues. Report urgent concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact mechanism rather than posting identifying information publicly (a dedicated protected reporting channel remains to be established).
