# HPI governance — provisional public operating rules V0.2

**Draft policy for a non-incorporated public-interest initiative, 5 November 2026.** These rules explain how the current repository is operated; they are **not** the constitution of a registered legal entity, evidence of an elected board, or an independently approved research governance system. They require public/community challenge before final adoption. The repository belongs to a single individual GitHub account until any formal transfer is approved and completed.

## 1. Purpose, standing and public accountability

HPI is a working name for open research aimed at reducing poverty and expanding equitable prosperity, with long-term research into economic systems. It does **not** currently collect donations, employ contributors, sponsor a regulated study, provide social services, or process participant records. Public documents must clearly distinguish published plans, candidate methods and measured real-world results. None of the last two are established merely by having a website.

## 2. Provisional roles and limits

| Role | Current status | May do | May NOT independently authorise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder / repository administrator | Existing single-account technical custody | Manage GitHub access, branch protection, issue triage, accepted technical changes and public documentation, with recorded rationale | Claim independent evidence approval, use participant data, sign commitments for a nonexistent foundation, override an ethical stop, suppress negative evidence |
| Contributors and public reviewers | Open invitation; no formal membership or appointed trustees | Propose evidence, corrections, translations, public methods and accessible criticism | Speak as legally authorised HPI representatives, commit project funds, recruit study participants |
| Scientific advisory reviewers | **Not yet appointed** | If later recruited with explicit role/conflict disclosures, challenge methods, pre-registration, uncertainty, harm and replication | Rewrite results to suit founder, sponsor or partner preference |
| Community/lived-experience reviewers | **Not yet appointed** | If later safely recruited, challenge relevance, burden, access barriers, distributional harm and informed participation | Be treated as symbolic endorsement or asked to expose identifiable hardship publicly |
| Legal/ethics/data steward | **Not appointed** | Following appointment and appropriate expertise, review lawful authority, consent, safeguarding and data processing for a concrete study | Provide retrospective legitimacy to unapproved data collection |

A developer merge or AI-assisted recommendation is **not** an independent researcher sign-off. Do not announce named advisers, a board or a fiscal sponsor until real parties accept in writing and conflicts are checked.

## 3. Decision categories and records

1. **Operational/documentary** (fixes, accessible site changes, citation corrections): reviewed PR, green required CircleCI and Vercel checks, reasoned commit and issue link; sensitive details redacted.
2. **Research methods/results** (outcome changes, eligibility, comparisons, risk claims, interpretation): proposal plus source evidence, alternative methods, conflict declarations and explicit version decision; independent challenge before a real-world method becomes approved. Preserve negative evidence and study deviations.
3. **Ethical/participant safeguards**: **hard stop** until a competent independent ethics/safeguarding decision, local legal/data-processing assessment and a responsible partner are established. Founder preference cannot waive a legal or non-negotiable safety condition.
4. **Mission/charter/legal/financial commitments**: public proposal and review; attributable founder/eligible governance approval, then formal legal advice or establishment as applicable. No unilateral public claim that HPI is a charity or sponsor.

For material decisions use the [decision record template](docs/governance/decision-template.md): date, proposer, type, reviewed sources, options, unknowns, recorded dissent, conflicts/recusals, decision owner and review date. Archive rather than rewriting public scientific history. Emergency removal of published private data/security incidents is permitted immediately; preserve a sanitized incident record.

**No silent suppression:** results and limitations must be published, including null, adverse or inconsistent outcomes, within privacy and licensing constraints. Corrections should link to the original analysis and reason.

## 4. Conflict-of-interest and independence protocol

Before anyone reviews or decides a material research/partnership/funding proposal:
- Declare financial, professional, employment, ownership, intellectual, personal and relevant institutional conflicts; **absence of a disclosed conflict is not proof of independence**.
- Record nature of relevant conflict and mitigation in the decision log while minimising unnecessary personal information.
- Separate drafting from independent methodological review whenever possible; flag founder-only decisions clearly.
- A conflicted decision-maker must abstain from high-risk or material decisions; a quorum of genuinely unconflicted reviewers is needed once formal decision bodies exist. If no unconflicted reviewers are available, **defer** the decision instead of treating silence as approval.
- Prohibit undisclosed sponsor control over analytic decisions, publication of negative findings, participant eligibility or consent materials.
- Allow affected contributors to respond to allegations, with privacy-safe recordkeeping.

The current founder-operated repository has **no formal board and thus no trustee quorum**; the above is an interim proposed standard, not a statement of charity trustee powers. Charity Commission [CC29 conflict guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/identifying-and-managing-conflicts-of-interest-in-a-charity-cc29) and [structure guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/charity-types-how-to-choose-a-structure-cc22a) inform later legal design, not proof of HPI charity status.

## 5. Community participation and criticism

Welcome non-academic lived experience, independent research review, multilingual critique and accessibility corrections. Discussions should be respectful, reasoned, and not extractive. Review and consultation can be public **only for non-sensitive proposals**; do not ask people to post benefit claims, diagnoses, addresses, income records or real participant stories in open Issues. Before any consultations with potentially vulnerable participants, publish an ethically approved accessible plan with reasonable non-digital routes, local collaborators and a private reporting/safeguarding pathway. No one is currently appointed to accept confidential case reports.

Moderation decisions should cite a rule and offer a proportionate appeal, without republishing personal details. The [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies. Material conflicts, critical viewpoints and contradictory science are not grounds for deleting a valid contribution.

## 6. Sensitive concerns and incident escalation

**Current channel is NOT ready for participant welfare or personal data:** no verified dedicated private safeguarding inbox/portal, recipient rota or data retention policy exists. Do not present ordinary public GitHub Issues or Discussions as a safe place for sensitive concerns. For suspected repository-security vulnerabilities use any verified **private vulnerability-reporting mechanism** separately when enabled; this is not a replacement for a welfare/safeguarding process.

Before outreach/real-world fieldwork, appoint an accountable human recipient; secure a restricted-access communication channel; define jurisdiction, emergency referral limits, non-collection policy, access logs, retention/deletion, response times and escalation to competent independent authorities. Never assert professional 24/7 support without staffing.

## 7. Data, publication and research ethics

Public Git may contain **only lawfully publishable, non-personal research metadata and original open materials**. Sensitive records, contact details, consent logs or actual participant microdata must not be committed to public issue histories or databases without a separately reviewed architecture and legitimate purpose. Consent to study participation is separate from the legal basis for personal-data processing; consult [ICO research principles](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/principles-and-grounds-for-processing/) and local law. Independent ethics review, data controller/processors, retention and data subject rights must be considered before any real project.

For evidence, record provenance, versions, uncertainties, third-party licence rights and reviewer conflicts. Publish aggregate/suppressed outputs only where lawful and non-identifying. AI-generated summaries are suggestions for human scrutiny, not independent scientific peer review.

## 8. Financial/legal boundaries

No donation, public fundraising or contract on behalf of a nonexistent HPI entity. Any proposed spending/funding must first have a real accountable legal party, authorisation limits, transparent bookkeeping, relevant taxes/regulation and declared conflicts. HPI has no adopted legal form; compare feasible structures only when activity requires them (e.g. an English/Welsh CIO, charitable company or other jurisdiction-appropriate form). Never assume an unincorporated association has legal personality or automatically limits personal liability; legal advice and actual charity-purpose eligibility are prerequisites.

The [F0-04 legal options brief](docs/governance/legal-options-brief.md) is exploratory, not legal advice or a decision to incorporate.

## 9. Versioning, transfer and review gates

The founding account administers the repository pending transparent governance transfer, should an appropriate independent entity be formed. Transfer needs documented ownership, finances/IP/licensing, governance and access controls, preserving public history. Until then, **provisional V0.2** can inform technical operations but is not independent approval of HPI Charter V1.0 or a future study.

Open gates: appropriate conflict review and community consultation; independent scientific method review; private safeguarding channel and human owner; competent legal/charity/insurance/data advice before sponsorship or funds; actual appointments with consent. See [F0-01 #10](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/10), [F0-04 #6](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/6) and [F0-03.1 #19](https://github.com/saver1991/human-prosperity-initiative/issues/19).

**Current verdict:** public provisional operating rules are documentable and reviewable; an independent charity board, safe sensitive-case reporting service, legal entity and ethics committee are **not established**.
