> **Working name · Early-stage public initiative · Not yet a registered foundation or legal entity**

**Vision:** A world where prosperity is possible for everyone.

**Mission:** Advance open, evidence-based approaches that reduce poverty and expand people's genuine opportunities to live secure, dignified lives—while studying the economic mechanisms that shape these outcomes.

**Current stage (November 2026):** Foundational research and community building. We have **not** run a field trial, proved an intervention, raised funds on behalf of a foundation, or established a legal charity. Any proposed timelines are targets, not promises.

## Two connected horizons

1. **Near-term (priority, ~0–5 years):** Identify practical poverty-related barriers, evaluate promising interventions with qualified partners, measure outcomes, and share what works *and what fails*.
2. **Long-term (~5–20+ years):** Investigate structural drivers of poverty and opportunity; test alternative economic arrangements using transparent evidence and reproducible models.

The second informs the first; the first gives empirical grounding to the second. We are **not** advocating a predetermined economic ideology or claiming that one model can solve every problem.

## How we measure prosperity

Prosperity is multidimensional: economic security; access to essential needs; health and education; real opportunities; personal autonomy, safety and long-term sustainability. We will build on established metrics, including the World Bank's poverty measures and UNDP's multidimensional poverty index, rather than claim a new metric is already validated.

Our aim is **measured improvement in people's lives**, not stars, followers, publications, or numbers reached. A participant reached is **not automatically** a participant helped.

## Current research — F0-02

Our initial rapid evidence scan covers three possible areas:

- **Access to existing social benefits and services** (administrative burden and non-take-up).
- **Affordability of essential needs** (initially household energy and related costs).
- **Economic opportunities** (better jobs, skills, economic-inclusion programmes).

See [F0-02 opportunity assessment](research/F0-02-opportunity-assessment.md) and the [evidence ledger](research/evidence-ledger.csv). **This is an initial scoping exercise, not a completed systematic review or an intervention recommendation.** The first field study depends on independent ethics review where appropriate, partners, and legitimate access to data.

## Join the initiative

Contributions are welcome from economists, social scientists, public-sector practitioners, designers, engineers, data scientists, NGOs and community members. You do **not** need to be an academic. Start a GitHub Discussion to introduce yourself and explain the area in which you can help. To propose evidence, use the issue template; to improve documents or code, open a pull request.

Please never publish identifying participant data, private records, credentials, or unpublished third-party datasets.

## Open knowledge and future foundation

The long-term aspiration is to establish an independent public-interest foundation **if** there is sufficient community support, governance capacity and legal/financial feasibility. This repository is currently held by the founding GitHub account, not an incorporated foundation. Organisational form and public accountability will be discussed openly before incorporation.
