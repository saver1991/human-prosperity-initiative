# Contributing to Human Prosperity Initiative

Thank you for considering a contribution. We welcome lived experience, research expertise, methodological criticism, data engineering, translation, design, accessibility, operations and ethical reflection.

## Where to begin
1. Read [README](README.md), [charter](docs/charter.en.md), [research standards](research/research-protocol.md) and [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
2. Use GitHub **Discussions** for exploratory ideas or introductions. Use **Issues** for a defined problem or new evidence.
3. For document/code changes: fork the repo, create a focused branch, and open a PR explaining what changed and why.
4. For research submissions, include source URL/DOI, publication date, geography, study design, outcomes, limitations and competing evidence. Note whether the material is peer-reviewed.
5. Ask questions before making a claim that could influence real-world vulnerable populations.

## Quality bar
- Cite empirical claims and distinguish evidence from speculation.
- Acknowledge uncertainty; seek disconfirming evidence.
- Avoid ideology-driven assertions and unsupported declarations of 'proven' interventions.
- Do not post personal data, sensitive records, proprietary content or copyrighted full texts without permission.
- Do not solicit funds or claim affiliation with a registered foundation.
- Use English for international reach; Italian and other languages are welcome, especially for translations and community research.
- Contributors retain their rights but grant the repository's stated documentation/code licences for their original contributions when merged; external material requires permission or linking.

**No paid position, formal affiliation or guaranteed response time is currently offered.**

## Research-source rights, ethics and accessibility checklist

For any research tooling, evidence, dataset metadata, international translation or scientific claim, the PR description must make the following traceable. A checkbox is an engineering declaration, **not** an independent ethical or legal approval.

- [ ] I authored the original content or cited the third party; I have identified any dependency, journal article, dataset, image and its actual licence. HPI's MIT software licence and CC BY 4.0 documentation licence do **not** confer rights to external content.
- [ ] I used **synthetic examples or public metadata only**. I have not committed raw or pseudonymised participant records, poverty case stories, secrets, private files, identifying small-cell aggregates or restricted papers.
- [ ] The relevant source, source revision or SHA-256, geographic scope, dates, conflicts of interest, data rights and uncertainty are recorded. Any missing fact remains explicitly **unknown**.
- [ ] I provided commands/fixtures so another contributor can reproduce the software check, and documented null, negative, adverse or missing results.
- [ ] All statistical or simulation results are labelled **descriptive/synthetic** until qualified **independent** methodological review. A passing build, CI or published PR is not a field result or experimental approval.
- [ ] I considered screen-reader semantics, keyboard navigation, translation and access without smartphones or high-bandwidth internet; automated Playwright/axe checks are not a substitute for real human assistive-technology testing.
- [ ] I documented research risks, privacy, informed consent, safeguarding and ethical gates for any proposed real-world use. No individual eligibility decision, outreach, data transfer, study recruitment or grant/funding solicitation is authorised by a code contribution.
- [ ] If correcting a published finding, I prepared a transparent, dated, append-only correction linked to the earlier public version. Negative evidence is retained and not silently removed.

**Public governance reference:** [RT-01.4 Privacy, Accessibility and Responsible Research](docs/RT-01-4-responsible-research-and-accessibility-v1.md), also available on the [HPI site](https://human-prosperity-initiative.vercel.app/research/responsible-tools). HPI remains unincorporated and its independent ethical, legal, scientific and accessibility sign-offs are still pending.
